Use your camera or upload a photo. Face the camera straight, plain background works best. Nothing is uploaded — everything happens on your device.

2 · Frame it

Drag the photo to position your face inside the frame. Use the slider to zoom. Head should fill most of the frame, eyes roughly on the upper third line.
